The resources mentioned in my talk *AI Made Every Developer a Tech Lead. Now what?*, plus some extra.

## Introduction

- [The Trident Model of Career Development](https://www.patkua.com/blog/the-trident-model-of-career-development/) · Patrick Kua · the four responsibilities
- [State of Tech Talent Report 2026](https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2026) · SignalFire · flat orgs, the Super IC

## Monday · WHAT to build

- [Inspired](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/35249663-inspired) · Marty Cagan · outcomes over outputs
- [What Do Engineers Mean When We Say "Taste"?](https://davegriffith.substack.com/p/what-do-engineers-mean-when-we-say) · Dave Griffith · works vs fits

## Tuesday · WHICH tradeoffs

- [Fundamentals of Software Architecture](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/44144493-fundamentals-of-software-architecture) · Mark Richards, Neal Ford · the First Law, -ilities

## Wednesday · WHERE boundaries go

- [Domain-Driven Design](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/179133.Domain_Driven_Design) · Eric Evans · bounded contexts
- [Introducing EventStorming](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/28947184-introducing-eventstorming) · Alberto Brandolini · architect as facilitator

## Thursday · WHO builds it

- [Staffing and Procurement for Fast Flow](https://www.michael-ploed.com/blog/staffing-and-procurement-for-fast-flow) · Michael Plöd · build, delegate, buy

## Friday · WHY it feels so hard

- [Sociotechnical Architecture](https://www.agile-meets-architecture.com/2026/schedule) · Xin Yao · team and system as one
- [V noční tramvaji](https://www.kosmas.cz/knihy/568295/v-nocni-tramvaji/)  (CZ) · Václav Havel · living in truth
